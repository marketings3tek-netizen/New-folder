THE RV PHILOSOPHY

Built For Members Who Prioritize Progress.

RV Fitness World was established to raise the bar for fitness facilities in Tambaram and Selaiyur. We eliminated the frustrations of ordinary gyms: narrow aisles, broken equipment left unattended, and aggressive sales quotas replacing real fitness advice.

We designed an open-span strength facility on Agaram Main Road where members can train with authentic Precor commercial machinery, Olympic lifting bars, and uncompromised floor room.

Alongside our flagship floor, our Selaiyur network includes a specialized CrossFit Box and a dedicated AC Gym, providing focused training environments suited to your schedule.
